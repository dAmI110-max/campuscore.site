# Campus Core — Refund and Cancellation Policy

**Effective date:** 10 November 2026  
**Applies to:** Seller Premium subscriptions purchased through Campus Core (Paystack).

## Principles

- Protect customers from our errors and from duplicate charges.
- Protect Campus Core from refunds of benefits already used.
- Be specific and leave an audit trail.

## Situations and handling

| Situation | Handling |
|-----------|----------|
| **Cancellation** | Stops future renewal and reminders. Premium stays active until the end of the already-paid period. **No refund** of an elapsed or in-use period. |
| **Duplicate charge** (two successful payments for the same period) | Verified against our ledger → **full refund of the duplicate**. Claim with the Paystack payment reference. |
| **Paid but not activated** (Paystack confirms success, but Premium was not applied) | We attempt automatic reconciliation. If we cannot activate within a defined window → **full refund**. |
| **Failed / abandoned payment** | No Premium granted. If your bank debited you, the reversal is between you, your bank and Paystack. We will help locate the reference. |
| **Account suspended for rule violations** | No refund. If we suspend in error, we restore access and extend by the lost time. |
| **Extended outage caused by us** | We extend the paid period proportionally. |
| **Chargebacks / disputes** | Handled through Paystack’s process. We supply the ledger as evidence. Access may be paused while a dispute is open. |

## How refunds are issued

- Only through Paystack to the original payment method.
- Timing depends on the customer’s bank.
- Two-step internal admin approval.
- Refunds never exceed the amount actually paid.

## How to request a refund

Contact support with the Paystack reference and a short description of the issue. Eligible requests are limited to the cases listed above.

---

*This policy applies only to platform Premium subscriptions. Campus Core does not process student-to-student marketplace payments or hold funds in escrow.*
